# Banoffee Pie

A classic Banoffee Pie is a no-bake dessert with a biscuit crumb base, buttery caramel (often made from dulce de leche or condensed milk), sliced bananas, and whipped cream. It is finished with chocolate shavings, cocoa, or a dusting of instant coffee.

## Summary

- **Base:** crushed digestive biscuits mixed with melted butter, pressed into a pie dish and chilled.
- **Filling:** a thick layer of toffee/caramel spread over the base.
- **Fruit:** ripe banana slices arranged over the caramel.
- **Topping:** softly whipped cream piled on top.
- **Finish:** chocolate curls, cocoa powder, or grated chocolate.

## Key points

- Use ripe but firm bananas so they hold their shape.
- Chill the pie well before serving so it slices neatly.
- For deeper caramel flavor, use dulce de leche or homemade toffee.
- It is best assembled shortly before serving to keep the bananas fresh.

## Serving suggestion

Serve cold with coffee or tea for a rich, sweet dessert.
